## Overview
Gothenburg is Sweden's second city, on the west coast, with a canal-lined
centre and an archipelago of car-free islands offshore.

## Best time to visit
June to August for the archipelago and the outdoor seasons. December for the
Liseberg Christmas market.

## Getting around
Trams cover the city on one ticket. Ferries to the southern archipelago leave
from Saltholmen and take bicycles but not cars.

## Top things to do
- Ride the trams through Haga and stop for a cinnamon bun.
- Take the ferry to Styrso or Vrango in the southern archipelago.
- Visit Liseberg, the largest amusement park in the Nordics.
- Walk the Feskekorka fish market and the canal district.

## Good to know
Gothenburg is about three hours from Copenhagen by train, so it pairs with a
Denmark trip.
