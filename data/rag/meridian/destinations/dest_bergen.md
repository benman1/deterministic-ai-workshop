## Overview
Bergen sits between seven mountains on Norway's west coast and is the usual
starting point for the fjords.

## Best time to visit
May to September for fjord trips and the longest days. It is one of the
rainiest cities in Europe, so pack for rain in any month.

## Getting around
The centre is walkable. The Floibanen funicular climbs Mount Floyen in seven
minutes, and the light rail runs to the airport.

## Top things to do
- Walk the Bryggen wharf, the old Hanseatic trading houses.
- Ride the Floibanen funicular for the view over the harbour.
- Take the fish market at Torget in the morning.
- Day-trip to the Naeroyfjord, a UNESCO fjord, by boat and rail.

## Good to know
Bergen is the western terminus of the Bergen Railway from Oslo, one of the
scenic rail routes in Europe.
