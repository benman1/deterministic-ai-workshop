Skis, snowboards, golf clubs, and bicycles travel as oversized items and are
not covered by the standard checked-bag allowance.

A ski or snowboard bag is 55 GBP each way when booked in advance. A bicycle is
75 GBP each way and must be boxed. Oversized items must be declared at least 48
hours before departure, and space is not guaranteed without a declaration.

These charges are in addition to any checked bag, not instead of one.
