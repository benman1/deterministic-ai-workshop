[2023 REQUIREMENTS - NO LONGER IN FORCE]

Travellers to Bali were required to show proof of a completed primary COVID-19
vaccination course or a negative PCR test taken within 48 hours of departure.
Unvaccinated arrivals were subject to quarantine at a designated hotel.

Retained for historical reference only. Do not quote these requirements to
customers.
