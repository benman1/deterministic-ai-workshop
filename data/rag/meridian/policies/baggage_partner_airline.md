Where a Meridian package is flown by a partner airline, the operating carrier's
baggage rules apply, not Meridian's.

Partner cabin allowance is commonly 7 kg rather than 8 kg, and some partners
price a checked bag by weight band rather than per bag. Your confirmation names
the operating carrier for each leg. Check that carrier's own table before you
travel, because a bag that is free on a Meridian-operated leg can be chargeable
on the connecting one.
