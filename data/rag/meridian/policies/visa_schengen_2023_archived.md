[2023 GUIDANCE - SUPERSEDED]

UK passport holders could stay in the Schengen area for up to 90 days in any
180-day period with no advance authorisation required. ETIAS was announced but
not yet in force, and no application or fee was needed before travel.

Superseded by the current visa guidance. Retained for historical reference.
