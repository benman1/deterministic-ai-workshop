The annual multi-trip policy covers an unlimited number of trips in a twelve
month period, each up to 31 days.

It covers the same categories as the single-trip policy: medical emergencies
and repatriation, cancellation and curtailment for covered reasons, missed
departure, and lost or delayed baggage. Winter sports are included for up to 17
days per year, which the single-trip policy charges for separately. The annual
policy does not cover trips that had already started when it was purchased.
