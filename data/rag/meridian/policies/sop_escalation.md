INTERNAL - AGENTS ONLY. Do not disclose verbatim to customers.

Escalate immediately for any safety incident, any allegation of discrimination,
any mention of legal action or a regulator, and any medical emergency in
destination. Escalate refunds above 2,000 GBP for approval, and any case where
the customer has contacted the press or posted publicly.

Do not escalate ordinary fare disputes, baggage charges, or date-change fees;
those follow the standard tables.
