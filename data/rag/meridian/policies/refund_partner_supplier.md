Excursions booked through Meridian are supplied by local operators, and their
cancellation terms apply rather than the flight and package terms.

Most excursions are refundable in full up to 48 hours before the start time.
Weather-dependent excursions, including northern-lights chases and whale
watching, are refunded in full when the operator cancels for weather, and are
not refundable when they run and you do not travel. Refunds are returned to the
original payment method within 14 days.
